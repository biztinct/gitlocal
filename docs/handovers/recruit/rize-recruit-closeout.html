Rize · Hiring · programme close-out

Rize Recruit — what was built

Every line Rize asked for in the requirements workbook and the 28 September call, what Payobook Hiring did before, and what it does now. Eight phases, built and checked between 30 September and 1 October 2026, live on payobook.com, on Rize's own system and on the template every new client is copied from.

The short version. Rize's way of hiring is now the product's default: their stage names on a drag-and-drop board per role, nothing waiting on a sign-off except the offer, application forms in three languages the talent lead edits herself, candidates private until a recruiter shares them, interviews with scorecards and daily chasing, "signed" kept apart from "joined", channels with tracked links and an agency portal, and — in the last phase — every candidate email in English, Vietnamese and Bahasa Indonesia plus a list of automations the talent lead writes as sentences.

What still needs you. The decisions below, Google sign-in details from Rize's Google admin, and the email records from Rize's IT. Nothing on this page needs a developer to change a setting: every switch below is on a screen in Hiring set-up.
