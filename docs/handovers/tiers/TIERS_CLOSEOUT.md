## Account migration

The exact approved ids were revalidated immediately before writes. Migration
called `pb.access.set_account_type` through the existing business facade during
maintenance, preserving its refusal checks, role bookkeeping and audit trail.
The live Access screen was not bypassed with SQL writes.

Twelve demo logins became Employee accounts: ess3, ess4, ess6–ess10 and minor.demo
under `payobook.com`, plus chi.ta, kiet.lam, demo.joiner and demo.joiner2 under
`example.com`. Five employees were linked to their existing active portal users.
No passwords, logins or activation flags were changed.

Drivers and field staff retain internal accounts because their `/driver` page
still needs one. The migration's harmless-group allowlist therefore contains
only multi-company; the fence's broader harmless list is not reused. Active
roles also prevent migration, even when the interactive switch could end them.
Four local migration guard tests cover these boundaries.

Every converted account has exactly the portal group and passes the portal-only
rule. A fingerprint of all other users' login, active flag, account type, default
company and direct groups is unchanged. A final audit found no eligible remaining
conversions or missing joiner links on any of the five databases.
