How to read this report

The four verdicts

Verdicts judge the live Payobook cockpit — a capability that exists only as an un-surfaced standard Odoo module counts as Partial, not Present.

Present

Live in the branded cockpit and demonstrable today with little or no work.

Partial

The engine or a partial cockpit exists, but key sub-features (or the branded UI) are missing.

Not Built

No meaningful implementation — a ground-up build is required to demo it.

Needs WOW-UX

Overlay flag: functionally there, but the interface must be rebuilt to demo standard.

Executive Dashboard & Payroll Analytics

Needs WOW-UX Present

Employee & Master Data
2

Employee Master Management

Partial

What exists

Branded People & Contracts cockpits: personal/employment details, department & manager hierarchy, position, bank details, salary-to-tax/insurance mapping. pb_people · pb_contracts · hr_employee_vietnam.

Banking & Documents
3

AI Bank Account Validation (OCR + Approval)

Not Built

What exists

Only a bank-export audit log (payroll_analytics_approval/bank_export_log.py). No document upload, OCR extraction, or validation engine anywhere in the codebase.

What's missing

The entire feature: multi-format upload, OCR field capture (bank/branch/holder/account/IBAN/SWIFT), format & duplicate validation, name string-match, and the Employee→HR→Finance approval chain with version history.

Demo impact: A headline "AI" moment in the scope — highest-effort net-new build, highest wow ceiling.
Attendance, Field & Travel
4

Attendance Management

Needs WOW-UX Partial

What exists

Standard engines for check-in/out, GPS geo-pins, RFID and face recognition. hr_attendance + _geolocation/_rfid/sttl_face_attendance — none surfaced in the branded cockpit.

What's missing

Bulk import wizard, missing-punch workflow, late-arrival/early-departure rules, and multi-tier correction approvals — plus a branded cockpit over the raw modules.

Demo impact: Feeds payroll inputs — needs both a workflow layer and a cockpit to demo cleanly.
5

Driver GPS Check-in

Not Built

What exists

Attendance geo-pins capture lat/long at punch only. hr_attendance_geolocation.

What's missing

Route tracking, device context, selfie verification, and a manager live-map dashboard with team check-in history & availability.

Demo impact: Visually striking if built (live map) — net-new, moderate effort.
6

Business Trip Management

Not Built

What exists

Only generic hr_expense (submit/approve expenses) — no trip concept.

What's missing

Trip-request lifecycle (destination, purpose, timelines, estimated cost, advance cash, attachments) and the Employee→Manager→Finance→HR→Authorized approval chain.

Demo impact: Prerequisite for #7 — build the two together as one travel story.
7

Business Trip → Attendance Integration

Not Built

What exists

Nothing — depends on #6 as a prerequisite.

What's missing

Auto "Present" during trips, exclusion from missing-punch/absence exceptions, and per-diem/travel-allowance flow into payroll.

Demo impact: The "it just flows into payroll" moment — bundle with #6.

Leave Management

Needs WOW-UX Partial

Overtime Management

Needs WOW-UX Present

Not Built

Overtime Meal & Food Allowance

Partial

What exists

Payroll Processing

Present

Payroll Validation (AI Pre-checks)

Present

What exists

AI Pulse engine: MoM variance, anomaly detection (cost/headcount/OT/leave), plus a Shadow Parallel-Run reconciliation certificate. pb_payroll_ai_insights/payroll_ai_pulse.py · shadow_run.py.

What's missing

Explicit "duplicate payment" flag could be surfaced more prominently; otherwise covered.

Demo impact: The AI proof point of the whole scope — a genuine differentiator, ready.
14

Payroll Approval Workflow

Present

What exists

Sequential approval cockpit with staged states, pending queues, stored totals and one-click advance to Final Lock. pb_approval.

What's missing

Currently 2-tier (HR→GM) vs the scope's 3-tier Officer→HR→Finance — a configuration extension, not a rebuild.

Demo impact: Present; add the third tier to match the scope's named chain exactly.

Present
