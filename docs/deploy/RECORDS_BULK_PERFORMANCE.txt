Records bulk-update performance — November 2026

A 650-person employee import remained uncommitted after ten minutes. A
diagnostic stack showed Records writing an employee field while standard
chatter tracking computed employee work-location fields. The evaluation
phase correctly prefetched the whole roster, but that same roster travelled
into each individual write. Contract/version invalidation caused repeated
computation over the entire batch.

The change limits the employee and contract prefetch cohort to the current
record while executing a planned write. Evaluation keeps its efficient batch
reads. Field values, write order, real-user access checks, approval decisions,
bank-account linking and both chatter and per-value Records audits are kept.
The change has no customer names, tenant branches or new UI controls.

Regression coverage counts real tracked-field computations after repeated
invalidation rather than asserting a machine-dependent time threshold. It
also checks the saved values, unchanged control employee and per-value audit.

contract components, undo, imports, company isolation and access capabilities.
