Rize absence policy and application-data payroll — implementation release
Owner approval: 21 October 2026. Prospective Rize activation: 1 November 2026.

Scope
Generic functionality ships to payobook, payobook_template, p0clone and rize,
only upgrading already-installed modules. Client mapping and policy seed helpers
refuse any database other than rize. ABM is retired and out of scope.

Decisions
Contractual payroll is the default, with employee work-calendar standard days
and an optional scheme fixed divisor. Timesheet mode requires manager approval
and attendance reconciliation. Paid absence contributes once; unpaid/partial
absence reduces employer-paid day equivalents. Spreadsheet calculations retain
their existing branches. No prior result or opening balance is fabricated.

Application runs require explicit period completeness review, typed sources,
required-input coverage, and frozen evidence checked at payroll approval.
Pending adjustments cannot be certified as zero. Nonzero overtime must reach a
pay-affecting component. Vietnam adds 150/200/300 percent application OT using
the existing hourly basis and existing downstream tax routing; other categories
require explicit mapped treatment. Policy controls require prior employee and
manager approval evidence, timesheet evidence, 40 monthly / 200 annual hours;
300 annual requires verified industry authority in a new published edition.

Canonical ownership
The 480 reviewed inputs have 350 resolved-provider bindings (including eight
protected HR-only blood/religion bindings excluded from payroll snapshots),
85 explicit pending domain metadata bindings, 20 retained recurring contract
terms, 21 retained reviewed payroll controls, and four legacy spreadsheet-only
inputs. Exact existing values may fill empty canonical fields; conflicts remain
for review. Assets, exit cases, documents and probation use their native owners.
New mode, paid-day, settlement and Vietnam OT inputs are additional to the 480.

Policy
Detailed country clauses take precedence over summary tables. Current active
employees covered by the four approved Rize schemas have owner-confirmed
full-time eligibility. Versioned country editions and native country leave types
are seeded prospectively. Annual allocations/opening balances need reconciled
HR evidence; optional scheduled year openings require explicit enrollment.
Singapore leave cashout and every country's excess-leave recovery remain
pending HR/payroll tax-treatment confirmation. Ambiguous or unfunded leave
categories block only affected requests/inputs until reviewed. Terminal leave
settlement preserves allocation history and prevents duplicate payout.
