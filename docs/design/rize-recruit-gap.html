At a glance

The short version. Most of the mechanics Rize needs already exist in Payobook Hiring: requests with a budget check, referrals, interviews with calendar files and two reminders, panel opinions with a 24-working-hour clock, background checks, document collection, offers, a joining hand-off and a numbers screen with Excel export. What Rize is really asking for is a different shape: their own stage names shown as a drag-and-drop board per role, nothing that waits on a sign-off (except the offer), application forms they can edit themselves in three languages, a recruiter-owned privacy model, and a pause between "offer signed" and "joined". Those five shape changes drive most of the work; the rest is small deltas.

Two things to know before build. (1) The workbook and the call disagree in twelve places; the call is taken as the ruling and each is listed below for your confirmation. (2) Hiring runs on the payobook.com demo database. At the last check it was not yet installed on Rize's own database, so the first deploy step is an install, not an upgrade.
