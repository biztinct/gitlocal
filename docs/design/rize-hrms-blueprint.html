01

The big picture

Zoho People remains the employee master — people are created there, and their basic record flows into Payobook automatically. Payobook is the operations and money platform: everything that touches assets, evaluations, payroll, budgets and vendors happens here. Employees see one portal for their own stuff.

Zoho keeps
  • Employee master record & ID
  • Departments & reporting lines
  • Hiring & recruitment
  • Time, attendance & leave
  • Goal setting & reviews
  • HR communications
  • Learning & development
  • HR document collection
Shared journeys
  • Onboarding & orientation — Zoho creates the person; Payobook runs the buddy, laptop, access and check-in journey
  • Offboarding & exit — resignation can start either side; Payobook runs clearances, asset recovery and final pay
Payobook owns
  • Payroll, compensation & benefits
  • Asset management
  • Probation evaluations
  • Performance improvement (PIP)
  • Vendors & access control
  • Rewards & recognition
  • Budget management
  • Contract & intern management

Data flows one way at a time. Zoho → Payobook is live in this build: new joiners, changes and resignations arrive automatically (with a file-upload backup until the live hookup is switched on). Payobook → Zoho is on hold, as agreed — designed for, not built.

16

Decisions I need from you

Eight calls that shape the build. Each has my recommendation — a simple "go with all recommendations" answer works too.

D1When a contract is extended, keep one contract or start a new one?

Today the contract screen edits values in place (a rule you set earlier). Extensions and conversions are cleaner as a new linked contract, so history reads "contract 1 ended, contract 2 began". Probation end-date changes would still edit in place.

Recommended: new contract for extensions & conversions; in-place only for probation dates

D2One budget system, not five

Payobook has accumulated four budget-shaped features from earlier programmes. RIZE's budgets will be built on the strongest one (the department budget-vs-actual engine), and the others stay for their own niches but are not used for RIZE reporting. Also: the group-currency converter currently lives inside the demo package — it should be promoted into the real product.

Recommended: yes to both

D3"Calendar invites" arrive as email + attachment

There's no Google/Zoho calendar integration in this build. Day-1 intro calls and check-ins go out as emails with a standard calendar attachment — one tap adds it to any calendar. A live calendar integration can come later.

Recommended: accept for now

D4Org chart: build our own view

New hires should see the org chart on day one. I recommend building a clean Payobook org-chart panel in our own style rather than switching on an old stock widget that won't match the app.

Recommended: build our own

D5Does an employee see their own PIP?

Strict visibility is the rule (HR + Head HR per country). The open question is the employee themselves: I recommend they do see their own plan and check-ins — they have to acknowledge it anyway, and hidden PIPs create legal risk. A switch lets you turn this off.

Recommended: employee sees own plan, switchable

D6Every employee gets a login automatically

You said yes to day-one access for everyone — confirming the mechanics: the login is created the moment the employee record arrives from Zoho, and the credentials email is held until joining day.

Recommended: confirm as described

D7Welcome poster format

Without Slack, the welcome poster becomes a designed card in the day-one team email, generated from the fun intro form. Same content, different channel.

Recommended: accept email format

D8Sign off the "who is the boss" table

Section 14 fixes which system owns which information. Once you sign it off, it becomes the law of the build — any later change is a deliberate decision, not a drift.

Recommended: sign off as shown
