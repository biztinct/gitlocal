2

What we already have

All seven Rize Must-Have lines for L&D are live on payobook.com. The pieces sit in three places today: the Training tab under Talent (HR side), the Training tab under Insights (numbers), and the employee's own My training pages, which internal staff can only reach through the search bar.

Courses and lessons

Courses with video, document, article, picture and quiz lessons, drawn on our own pages. New courses are private and invitation-only. Lessons are marked done one by one and the progress bar moves at once.

Tests and the question bank

A test at the end of a course that stays locked until every lesson is done. Pass mark 70, three attempts, score shown at once, certificate on a pass. A question bank lists every question written.

Putting people on courses

Assign a course to one person, a department, a job or everyone, with a due date and a reason (day one, trial period, leadership programme, compliance, one-off). Only one open assignment per person per course.

Reminders and chasing

Email to the employee and manager at the start; reminders 3 days before, 1 day before, on the day, then every 3 days; manager warned 2 days late, HR lead 5 days late. "Ask for more time" with a reason, decided by the manager.

Comes round again, day one, trial period

Recurring schedules (every N months, by department or job) that never double-assign. Day-one rules for joiners by department or job. A trial-period course blocks probation confirmation until done.

Allowance and claims

A yearly training allowance per company with personal overrides. Employees claim an outside course back with receipt and certificate; HR agrees; it becomes a one-off amount the pay team adds to a pay run.

Certificates on the record

A passed test files the certificate PDF in the person's own document folder. The standard résumé line for a certification is written too.

Numbers

For any date range: finished, passed, past their date, still to do, average score, more time agreed; by department, course and reason; week-by-week trend; spreadsheet download; weekly or monthly emailed pack (built, switched off).

Manager view

"My team's training" shows a manager their direct reports' courses and progress. The new Home → Team page (live 21 Oct) also lists training requests.

Differentiators that would make a plant or estate manager stop

Practical sign-off on a phone

Absorb and SC Training let a supervisor open a checklist on their phone, watch the worker do the task, tick each step, add a photo and press Pass. The result counts as a completion like any test. This is the one feature that fits a factory floor or a field better than any video.

Phase L4

Live training matrix by site and job

A grid: people down the side, required courses and licences across the top, each cell green (valid), amber (expiring) or red (missing). Drillable and printable for an auditor. Only the frontline specialists ship this as a real grid; the big suites make you build it from reports.

Phase L2

Country compliance packs

Vietnam's safety-training decree sorts every worker into six groups with set hours and a yearly or two-yearly refresher; India requires a regular harassment-awareness session for everyone; Indonesia tracks named safety officers and operator licences; Singapore funds training by the hour. No product surveyed models the Vietnam rule natively. We can.

Phase L2

A changed procedure re-trains everyone

Publish version 2 of a safety procedure and every person who passed version 1 gets a short re-read and a three-question check. Rare in the market, cheap to build, exactly what a safety manager wants.

Phase L5

Toolbox talks and classroom sessions

A ten-minute on-site talk logged by the supervisor with attendance, counting toward hours and compliance. Classroom sessions with a QR code at the door for attendance.

Phase L4

Did it work? Asked 30 days later

After a course: a two-minute feedback survey. Thirty days later: the manager is asked whether behaviour changed. Only SAP and Docebo attempt this; most HR platforms stop at completion.

Phases L5, L6

What we deliberately leave out, and why

  • Outside catalogue connectors (Go1, Udemy, LinkedIn Learning). Paid outside services; the standing rule is outside services off unless Rize asks. We build our own outside-course list with links and costs first.
  • Leaderboards. Rejected by the quiet-board rule: colour and competition only where someone must act. Badges on certificates are fine; a ranking of colleagues is not (decision 7).
  • Gating the clock-in on training (Rippling's "cannot clock in until certified"). Powerful but it stops people being paid. Listed as a later option, not in these eight phases.
  • Full offline app. Needs a native app. Phase L7 gives phone-first pages that survive a dropped signal mid-lesson; a true offline mode is a later decision (decision 6).
5

The design, screen by screen

Every screen below follows the Payobook rules: the quiet-board pattern (grey at zero, colour only where someone must act), plain words on every label, Lucide icons, a named hero moment, and no dead ends. Each screen is also designed for a phone, because most of Rize's learners hold a phone, not a laptop.

Overview (HR, training manager)

Hero moment: one sentence at the top in plain words, for example "92% of required training is in date. 12 certificates expire within 30 days. 4 people are past their due date." Below it, three quiet rows:

  • Waiting on you: claims to agree, more-time requests, course requests, sessions to confirm. Each opens a drawer; nothing leaves the page.
  • Compliance at a glance: required training in date by site, department and country, with the gap count. Click a tile to open the Required matrix filtered to it.
  • This month: sessions coming up, courses due, certificates expiring, renewals sent.

States: empty (no courses yet: a single "Create your first course" card with three starter templates); nothing waiting (grey tick, "All clear"); large (counts cap at 99+ and link to the full list).

My learning (everyone)

Hero moment: the next thing to do, as one big card: the lesson name, how long it takes, one button "Continue". On a phone this fills the screen. Below: filters Assigned · In progress · Done, then my path (where I am on it), my certificates and licences with valid-until dates, my allowance and claims.

  • "Done" is the person's learning record: every course, session and sign-off with the date, hours and score. Downloadable as one PDF for a job move or an audit.
  • A certificate about to expire shows an amber "Renew by 14 Nov" chip and a "Start renewal" button that opens the refresher course.
  • "Ask to take a course" opens a short form: course or link, cost, why, when. The manager decides; HR sees the budget effect before agreeing.
  • Managers get a "My team" tab here: each report, their status, one-tap "Remind".

Courses

The library as cards with a cover, length, lessons, test, who it is for and a tag: Ours (online), Classroom (a course delivered in sessions), Outside (a provider course with link and cost). Filters by job, department, country and skill. Opening a course shows its lessons, test, sessions, versions, who is on it, and the feedback score.

  • Versions: "Publish a new version" marks every holder as needing a re-read; the course page shows how many are still on the old one.
  • Sessions tab on a classroom course: dates, place, trainer, seats, waitlist, attendance.
  • Outside course: provider, cost, link, hours; "Ask to take it" for an employee, "Put someone on it" for HR, which pre-books the allowance.

Paths

Hero moment: the path drawn as a line of stations, like a rail map, each station a course, session, sign-off or outside course, with the share of people at each station. A path has a purpose tag: Role (new agronomist, line supervisor), Career (operator to team leader), Skills (forklift, boiler, pesticide handling). Steps can be "must finish before" or "any order". A path can be auto-given to a job or department on day one, and linked from a growth plan in Talent.

Assigned

The existing assignments board, kept as is: who is on what, due when, why, how far; More time requests; bulk assign by person, department, job or everyone; "Send the reminders now". Adds: one-tap nudge per row, and a path column so a step inside a path reads the same as a one-off course.

Required

Hero moment: the training matrix. Rows are people, columns are required items, cells are green, amber or red. Filter by site, department, job, country, safety group. Click a red cell: "Assign now". Print or download for an auditor. Behind it, the rules: "Everyone in job X at site Y must hold course C, valid N months" plus the country packs (Vietnam safety groups 1–6 with hours and refresher cadence; India annual awareness session; Indonesia licence register; Singapore hours). Day-one rules and "Comes round again" schedules move here as two kinds of rule.

Tests

Tests, question bank, and practical sign-offs (checklists). A test gets a results page: pass rate, score spread, the questions most people get wrong, retake policy. Random draw from a question pool: "ask 10 of these 30". A sign-off checklist is a list of steps with Pass / Fail per step, a photo and a note, done on the supervisor's phone by scanning the worker's QR code or picking the name.

Certificates

One register of every certificate and licence a person holds: from our tests, from classroom sessions, from outside providers (uploaded with a photo), and statutory cards. Each has issued-on, valid-until, issuer and the file. Filters: expiring in 30 / 60 / 90 days, expired, by site and job. Renewal reminders go to the person at 60, 30 and 7 days, the manager at 14 days, HR at expiry, and the refresher course is assigned automatically when one exists.

Skills (later phase)

A course or sign-off grants a skill at a level. A job requires skills at levels. The page shows each person's skills against their job, the gap, and the course that closes it. Feeds the manufacturing skills paths and the workforce-planning gap view that already reads skills.

Claims

Unchanged from today: allowance, claims waiting, agreed, refused, paid. Adds the pre-approved course request on the claim so the receipt arrives against something already agreed.

Numbers

The existing Training numbers page, extended with: training hours per person per period, cost (course and session costs plus claims against the allowance and the budget line), compliance by site and country, feedback scores and the 30-day manager follow-up, and the statutory exports (Vietnam renewal list due in 30 days; India attendance register; Singapore hours by course). The weekly or monthly pack carries the same.

6

The new building blocks, in plain words

Seven things the system cannot say today and will be able to say after the build. Each is one new record type or one new rule engine, described by the question it answers.

A certificate record

"What does this person hold, who issued it, and until when?" One row per certificate or licence, however it was earned. Expiry drives reminders and re-assignment.

A requirement rule

"Who must hold what?" Job, department, site, country or safety group, maps to a course, path or licence with a validity. The matrix is the rules evaluated against the people.

A learning path

"In what order?" An ordered list of steps with prerequisites, a purpose tag, and progress per person.

A session

"When, where, with whom?" Date, place, trainer, seats, waitlist, attendance, hours. A toolbox talk is a short session logged after the fact.

A practical sign-off

"Can they actually do it?" A checklist template and a signed result on a phone with a photo.

An outside course and a course request

"What can people take elsewhere, and did we agree before the money went?" A catalogue entry and an approval that pre-books the allowance.

A feedback and follow-up survey

"Was it any good, and did it change anything?" Sent at completion and 30 days later to the manager; scores land on the course and in Numbers.

8

Decisions needed from you

Eight questions, each with a recommendation. Decisions 1 and 2 block phase L0; the rest can be answered when their phase comes up.

1. What do we call the two menu entries?
Recommended: the new area is "Learning" (Vietnamese "Học tập"); the existing how-to-use-Payobook entry is renamed "Guide" (Vietnamese "Hướng dẫn").
Alternatives: call the new area "Training" and leave "Learn" alone (cheapest, but Rize asked for Learning and the word Training will sit on a rail item anyway); or rename the guide "Help". Renaming touches the lessons and tours that mention "Learn", which the standing rule says we update in the same phase.
2. Does Training leave Talent completely?
Recommended: yes. Talent keeps Goals, Growth and Praise. A growth plan links to a learning path, so the two areas still meet.
Alternative: keep a "Training" shortcut on Talent that jumps to Learning. It costs a rail slot on Talent and two places to look for one thing.
3. Which country packs first?
Recommended: Vietnam safety groups first (Rize's largest workforce and the most prescriptive law), then India's annual awareness session, then the Indonesia licence register, then Singapore hours.
Each pack is rules plus a report. The Vietnam one needs Rize to tell us each worker's safety group, or a rule that derives it from the job.
4. Practical sign-off on a phone: in this build?
Recommended: yes, in L4. It is the feature that makes Learning real on a factory floor and no mid-market HR platform has it.
It needs supervisors to have the app open on a phone. If Rize's supervisors do not carry phones at work, it becomes a tablet at the line, which still works.
5. Outside courses: our own list, or a paid catalogue connector?
Recommended: our own list with links and costs now; a connector to a paid catalogue only if Rize asks and pays for the subscription.
Connectors (Go1, Udemy, LinkedIn Learning) are outside services with their own contracts. SCORM import, the way bought courses arrive as a file, is cheaper and is listed under L7.
6. How far do we go on the phone?
Recommended: phone-first pages that keep their place when the signal drops (L7). A true offline app is a later, separate decision.
A real offline mode means a native app and a sync engine. Rize's field agronomists would benefit, but it is a product of its own.
7. Badges and points?
Recommended: a badge on each certificate and a completion streak on My learning; no leaderboards.
Leaderboards rank colleagues and break the quiet-board rule. Frontline specialists use them heavily, so this is a taste call.
8. PayAI drafts courses from a document?
Recommended: yes, in L7: upload a procedure, get an outline, lessons and a ten-question quiz to edit. Every product surveyed added this in 2025.
It only drafts; a trainer always edits and publishes. Uses the PayAI engine already live.

Engineering notes

For the handover writer and the implementing agents. Verified against the repository on 21 Oct 2026. Plain-English rules above bind every user-visible string; this section is the only place with internal names.

Standing rules that bind every phase

  • White label: no "Odoo" in any user-visible string (labels, help, selection values, mail, .po). Technical identifiers untouched.
  • Rail standard (NAV ledger R1/R2): 72 px sticky rail, 60 px buttons, 18 px icon over a 9 px uppercase label, ≤2 lines EN and VI, ten items target, twelve hard max, counts only on queue items, no hover-widening.
  • NAV ledger R7/R8: Learn lessons, coaching, tours and training replicas updated in the same phase as any menu change (bump pb_learn); Access and delegation screens show the new menu after every phase.
  • Design mandate: hero moment named per screen, zero dead ends, Lucide icons only, motion with purpose, quiet-board pattern, browser validation with Chrome before "done".
  • Deploy: one addons directory, tiered deploy rule, payobook-first, check /opt/deploy-notes.md, announce restarts to peer sessions, warm asset bundles after restart.
  • Money: nothing in Learning writes a payslip. Agreed claims and pre-approved requests become pb.incentive awards on the one-off lane, as today.

Non-goals for this programme

  • No native mobile app or offline sync engine.
  • No paid catalogue connectors unless the owner contracts one.
  • No clock-in gating on training status.
  • No change to how a claim becomes money: the one-off award lane stays the only door.
  • The product guide (pb_learn) keeps its content and behaviour; only its menu label changes.
