Contractual default · Approved timesheets optional

Recommended for Rize

  • Contractual by default. Start with the eligible standard month, then adjust unpaid or partially paid absence. Paid leave is already included.
  • Calendar-based standard days. Use each employee’s working calendar; allow an effective-dated fixed divisor per schema.
  • Optional approved-time basis. Manager-approved timesheets, reconciled to attendance, plus paid absence and eligible holiday credits.
  • Application data first as an explicit run option. Keep spreadsheet import and the existing source-priority settings for uploaded runs.

Build on what Payobook already has

Keep Formula Studio, Mapping Studio and the four-step payroll wizard. Extend their existing source resolver with approved period metrics and related-record providers.

Use native leave allocations, timesheet approvals, overtime requests, assets, assignments and offboarding cases. Avoid a second employee master or a parallel leave balance.

“Run without a spreadsheet” already exists. The new work makes its inputs complete, period-aware and explainable.
Live readiness finding: there are no timesheet packets or overtime requests in Rize. Its 4,371 work entries are draft; the three approved leave requests are from July–August 2026. These counts do not establish October completeness. Application-only payroll needs a reviewed opening balance, calendar assignment and monthly certification before absence of records can mean zero.

What happens when the operator clicks Run

01 · Resolve

Freeze the employee population, employment slices, calendars, policy edition, mappings and cutoff. Resolve only sources allowed for this run.

02 · Explain

Show each required value as resolved, confirmed zero, missing, stale or conflicting. Separate payroll blockers from incomplete profile metadata.

03 · Calculate

Create an immutable input snapshot and draft results. If approved source records change before approval, mark the draft stale and require a reviewed refresh. Review exceptions and net-pay changes before approval. The action does not release payment.

Precise calculation contract

Contractual: eligible contractual units E − unpaid loss equivalents U − partial-pay loss equivalents Q. Full-pay leave remains within E. Base salary = monthly salary × payable units ÷ divisor D. For a full month on a fixed basis, E = D. This POC consistently converts a scheduled-day fraction to standard units using D / scheduled days in BOTH modes; one unpaid scheduled day on the 30/22 example is 1.3636 standard units. A direct salary/30 deduction convention is a separate country rate rule and must be approved explicitly, including an all-unpaid-month rule. Do not mix a 30-day numerator with a 22-day denominator.

Approved time: approved regular work + paid-leave equivalents + eligible paid holiday credits. Unpaid time contributes zero; do not deduct it again. Overtime is separate from ordinary paid units. For a fixed monthly divisor, normalize approved scheduled-day equivalents to the eligible standard-unit scale before applying D; pure hourly contracts use their hourly rate. The demo uses normalization to 30/22 in both modes, preserving equivalent pay for the same approved events and zero pay for a wholly unpaid month.

Variable schedules: compute day fractions using each day’s scheduled hours. The demo’s 8-hour conversion is illustrative. Clip daily time to the payroll period and local work date; split overnight work, transfers, salary changes and join/exit slices consistently. Apply employment proration once across the resolver and existing proration engine.

Zero and missing: numeric 0 is a value. A blank spreadsheet cell falls back; it does not mean zero or clear. Unknown coverage is missing even when an event query returns no rows. Required unresolved salary, calendar, time or deduction blocks calculation; optional display-only fields do not.

Turn policy clauses into behavior

Eligibility, evidence and approvals
  • Version rules by company/country and effective date; retain source page and interpretation. Joining date comes from the legal employment episode, not the latest renewed contract.
  • Annual allocation and carryover are ledger entries with once-only keys; expiring excess needs a posted balance transaction, not only a warning. Import reviewed opening balances with a cutover date.
  • Wellness leave over 3 consecutive days needs a medical certificate; HR may request it earlier. First-day manager notification and post-return application are supported. Certificates stay restricted to appropriate HR roles.
  • Maternity requests retain birth/event dates, expected delivery, surviving-child/multiple-birth conditions, country lead-in limits and supporting evidence. Employee remains active for performance and variable payout eligibility. If resignation occurs without returning, benefit ends on last employment day.
  • Paternity: male employees, 14 calendar days, maximum two births/adoptions, taken within one year in no more than two blocks; no accumulation or encashment. Adoption: 84 calendar days for eligible women in India/Singapore with child under one; no carry or cash-out.
  • Singapore childcare: child under 7, Singapore citizen, at least 3 continuous months’ service; 6 days. Marriage: 3 days once a year. India LTA claim requires at least 4 consecutive PL working days, excluding Sundays/holidays.
  • Preserve manager email approval evidence during transition. Proposed portal workflow records the same authority and HR Head/CEO exceptions; replacing the mandatory email + Google Form steps needs the process-owner’s approval.
  • Leaves during notice need manager approval. Leave extensions and insufficient balances route to review; do not turn a pending request into an approved unpaid deduction automatically.
Overtime in the policy

Vietnam: 8 hours/day and 48/week maximum normal hours unless otherwise agreed; employee and employer prior approval. Monthly cap 40 hours; annual cap 200, with 300 only for eligible industries. Weekday 150%, weekly rest day 200%, public holiday 300% excluding the ordinary holiday wage. Store requested, approved and actual hours separately, with email evidence and timesheet reconciliation. Payslips show hours, base and multiplier.

Classify by the employee’s actual weekly-rest calendar, not Saturday/Sunday alone. Weekday/rest-day/public-holiday categories are mutually exclusive base classes; night premiums are separate dimensions. Keep total-rate versus premium-only semantics explicit. Check period and year-to-date limits; payroll cannot erase worked-hour liability by dropping an over-limit record.

The PDF does not specify a complete night/extended OT rule set or non-Vietnam OT rates. Expose those mapping categories, but keep unsupported rate plans unconfigured until approved; never copy Vietnam rates to every country.

Literal interpretation and remaining ambiguity

Resolved by your precedence rule: India miscarriage is 42 continuous days under the detailed clause (the table says 45); India maternity is 26 weeks up to two surviving children and 12 weeks for the third, with the stated 8-week pre-birth limit. Vietnam maternity adds one month per additional child, and allows at most two pre-birth months.

Still needs HR interpretation: the maternity section permits PL/sick leave alongside maternity, then says additional leave and “any accumulated PL balance” become loss of pay. These are conflicting detailed clauses; do not confiscate balances or auto-convert PL. Route affected extensions to HR.

Indonesia bereavement: detailed general text grants 3 working days in lieu of Indonesian leave, while specific later clauses still point to 2-day family death and 1-day household death. Provide one event entitlement with no stacking, but require HR to settle this detailed-clause conflict before configuring a duration for that overlap.

Other unresolved details: the scope of the general new-joiner proration clause for wellness and special-event leave (do not automatically prorate childbirth entitlements); partial-month joining/exit rounding; Singapore 14 outpatient/60 hospitalization leave shared-cap relationship; whether Indonesia 2 menstrual days is per cycle/month; exact pay fraction and payer for each benefit; excess-leave deduction permissions; gross-salary component definition; use order of carried/current entitlement. No unsupported rule is presented as settled.

Research note, not automatically activated: MOM now documents eligible Singapore paternity at 4 weeks from April 2025 and shared parental leave at 10 weeks from April 2026. The PDF itself names Indonesia Law4/2024, but its three-month operational clause does not describe that law’s conditional extension and tiered pay; this is also an interpretation issue. These findings remain flagged for HR; this POC does not silently activate them. See official links in Evidence.

Mapping Studio should ask two questions

Read for this pay run: which approved business fact supplies this input?
On import: where may this value be written, if anywhere?

Related-record adapters need employee/company keys, stable business IDs, date selection and multiplicity rules. “Existing field” alone is not enough. Derived totals and approval statuses have no ordinary writeback destination.

Safe migration of existing contract data

  1. Capture current values, types, mapping version and provenance.
  2. Preview owner changes and conflicts against existing records.
  3. Copy only unambiguous approved values; hold conflicting/unknown semantics for review.
  4. Reconcile totals and record counts; keep original contract values as historical evidence.
  5. Switch future reads to the new provider; deprecate old storage after acceptance.

No mass “header looks similar” remapping; no deletion of historical contract data.
