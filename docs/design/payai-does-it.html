By typing or by speaking. With your rights only, with a plan card before anything happens, and with an undo afterwards.

What it may do

Every command has a colour. The colour decides what PayAI needs from you.

The colour is enforced on the server, not in the chat window. A company switch lets an admin turn each colour on or off.

Eight things that stop a mistake

Your rights, never more

PayAI runs the command as you. If you cannot press the button on screen, PayAI cannot press it for you. It never borrows admin power.

Guesses are visible

Every blank on the plan card says where it came from: you said it, you chose it, PayAI assumed it, or it is the default. Assumed ones are highlighted.

Real counts before you press

The plan card uses the same checks the screen uses: how many people are covered, whether a run already exists, who is not covered.

Plans expire and are re-checked

A plan lasts ten minutes. At the moment you press Do it, PayAI checks again. If a June run appeared meanwhile, it stops and tells you.

Undo for ten minutes

Amber actions get an undo button right on the card. Where undo is impossible, the card says so before you press.

Red needs a typed word

Approving, sending back and rejecting ask you to type a word. Voice never confirms a red action, so a stray "yes" in the room does nothing.

Everything is written down

Each request leaves a record: who asked, the words used, what PayAI understood, what you confirmed, what it created. It shows in the audit log and on the run's history.

A switch per company

Green, Amber and Red can each be turned on or off per company. Tenants start fully off, like the helper's ChatGPT answers today.

Voice

Speaking works the same way, with two extra habits

Hold to talk already exists in PayAI. Your words appear in the box and you press send. We keep that. Two additions make voice feel natural without making it risky.

It reads the plan back

"I will run June payroll for Retail End-Month, 902 people. Tap Do it or say go ahead." You hear what you are about to approve.

"Go ahead" works for Amber only

And only if the company switch allows it. Red always needs a tap or a typed word. Blocked stays blocked.

Something I found while mapping this

The Run Payroll screen has no server-side lock today

The menu hides the Run Payroll screen from people without the right. But the steps behind it do not check who is calling. Anyone who is signed in and knows the address can start a pay run. The "Clean and run again" step can also delete runs that are already approved or paid, with no record of it.

  • Nobody is likely doing this. It is a hole, not a leak we have seen.
  • An AI door would inherit the hole, so it must be closed first, on every database.
  • I would do this part myself, because it is security work. About a day.
