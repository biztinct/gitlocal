RIZE Wave 2 Blueprint

The five tabs that were going to stay in Zoho — Hiring, Goals, HR announcements, Leave & attendance, and Training — now come into Payobook, built the same way and to the same finish as the first ten. Every requirement row from your sheet is below, with what already exists, what gets built, where it shows up, who signs off, and which switches you control.

01

The big picture

Wave 1 made Payobook the operations and money platform for RIZE and put every sign-off under one Approval Matrix with one inbox. Wave 2 closes the remaining gap: the whole employee story — from the hiring request to the training certificate — now runs here. Zoho stays the employee master for names, IDs and reporting lines, exactly as before.

Live since wave 1
  • Onboarding & orientation
  • Offboarding & exit
  • Assets
  • Probation
  • Performance improvement
  • Pay packages, awards, payroll calendar
  • Rewards & recognition
  • Budgets
  • Contracts & interns
  • Vendors & access
Extended in wave 2
  • Leave & attendance — the Leave Command Center, missing-punch rules and the field check-in app already exist; the holiday calendar, escalations, carry-forward watch and field-staff eligibility are added
Built new in wave 2
  • Hiring & recruitment — request to offer to day one
  • Goals & OKRs — set, approve, lock, check in, score
  • HR announcements — calendar, scheduled sends, birthdays
  • Training — courses, tests, assignments, budget claims

Outside services stay switched off, as you ruled. No LinkedIn posting, no e-signature, no Google Calendar, no Slack. Everything goes by email with calendar attachments; a signed offer is recorded by hand. Every screen is built so the connection can be switched on later without redesign.

Hiring & recruitment

A function head raises the request; from there the recruiter runs the loop — posting, screening, interviews, feedback, BGV, offer — and the day the offer is signed the person drops straight into the same new-joiner journey a Zoho arrival gets.

Goal setting & OKRs

 New build, two steps. A goal cycle per financial year; each person writes goals with key results, the manager weights and approves, HR locks. Monthly check-ins keep it alive; scores roll up at year end. Employees do everything from their own page.

12

Decisions

Five you have already taken — recorded, not re-asked. Three small ones are left; each has my recommendation, and "go with all recommendations" works.

D10Extend leave, build the other four new

Leave & attendance gets its gaps filled inside the screens that exist. Hiring, Goals, Announcements and Training are built fresh in the RIZE style.

Decided 15 Sep

D11Build and test on the demo system

Everything lands on payobook.com first. Moving it to the RIZE tenant is a separate step afterwards, under the usual roll-out rule.

Decided 15 Sep

D12Outside services stay off

LinkedIn posting, e-signature, Google Calendar and Slack: email only, calendar attachments, screens ready to connect later.

Decided 15 Sep

D13Field attendance is location plus selfie

No face matching, no biometric data stored. The selfie is a photo on the attendance record, nothing more.

Decided 15 Sep

D14Reuse the two engines; keep the applicant store

Training runs on the installed E-Learning engine plus the Surveys test engine, with every learner screen ours. Hiring keeps the standard applicant, job and talent-pool tables underneath a bespoke request-to-offer layer. Rebuilding a video player, a quiz engine and a candidate pipeline would take months for a worse result.

Decided 15 Sep (delegated to the designer)

D15Install the test engine on the live demo system

Step E1 installs the Surveys engine (question bank, scored tests, certificates). It is a standard component, already used by thousands of installations, and its stock pages will be locked to internal users and swept for wording. This is the only module install in the wave.

Recommended: yes — approving this page authorises it

D16Who counts as "field staff" for check-in?

Today only drivers can use the check-in app. The sheet names agronomists. I recommend a group you fill yourself — start with agronomists and drivers — rather than a hard-coded job title, so it grows without a change request.

Recommended: a "Field staff" group, drivers + agronomists to start

D17Do birthday cards go out from day one?

The anniversary mail is off today because it reaches real inboxes. Birthdays would join it. I recommend both ship off and you turn them on together once the wording of the card has been seen.

Recommended: off at launch, one switch for both
