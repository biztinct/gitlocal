1 · Verdict

At the start, the training tools sat inside Talent and covered the basics. Learning is now its own top-level menu with 11 tabs, and most of what Rize asked for exists and works.

What this means: an employee, a manager and the training team can each do their whole training job inside Payobook: assign, learn, sit in a room, sign in by QR code, take a test, hold a licence, renew it, ask for a paid course, claim the money back, and see the numbers.
The one honest caveat: the work was checked live as the admin and through server-side checks as each demo person. It was not walked in each person's own browser, and a few things have not been tried on a real phone or with real AI. Section 6 names them.

5 · The Learning menu today

Eleven tabs, in this order. The maximum the menu allows is twelve.

OverviewMy learningCourses Sessions · OutsidePathsAssignedRequiredTestsCertificatesClaims RequestsSkillsNumbers

For employees

  • One card for what to do next, plus a three-minute lesson.
  • Courses with due dates, paths step by step, sessions to book and sign into.
  • Certificates with a badge and the date they run out.
  • Ask for a course, claim the money back, rate a course.
  • Lessons work on a phone and keep their place.

For managers

  • Who on the team is late, with a one-tap reminder.
  • More-time and course requests to decide.
  • A short question a month after someone finishes a course.
  • Quiet counts on Home › Team.

For the training team

  • Courses, sessions, toolbox talks, outside courses, and PayAI drafts.
  • Paths, who owes what, who must hold what, tests and results.
  • The certificate register, claims, requests and skills gaps.
  • Numbers with a spreadsheet, and the Learning settings.

Rules the build keeps

  • Quiet boards: colour only where someone must act.
  • English and Vietnamese on every new screen.
  • No ranking of colleagues. Only the Payobook name on screen, never the platform underneath.
  • Agreed claims are paid as one-off amounts in a pay run, never written into a payslip.

6 · Every gap, sorted by who has to act

Nothing here blocks use of the menu. The groups tell you what needs a decision, what was a choice, what is unproven, and what is housekeeping.

A · Waiting on you

  • L7Try "Draft it with PayAI" on payobookEach draft spends a little AI credit, so nobody pressed it on live. Use a Word or text file: scanned PDFs cannot be read by payobook's AI provider.
  • L6Turn on the monthly report packIt is off on every database. Settings › HR › Learning › Report pack. Use the preview and "Send me a test" first.
  • L2Ask Rize which "To confirm" rules are rightThose country rules stay off until Rize confirms. HR switches each on in Settings › Learning › Country packs.
  • L6Tell ChatGPT to hold Learning releasesIts combined releases picked up Learning work despite a hold note, twice. The rule to give it: never ship the Learning modules until a line says "LEARNING … DONE".
  • L1When should email texts be in Vietnamese?All training emails are in English for everybody. Reminders, certificates, sessions, feedback.
  • L3Should a second path hold a step back?A course shared by two paths counts on both. A course someone was already late on keeps its date when a path adopts it.
  • L5Should paths ignore re-reads?Publishing a new course version reopens that step for people who must re-read it.
  • L6Can training managers change Learning settings?Today only the head of training can save them. Managers can read them.
  • L4Put the demo company on Vietnam time?The company is on Brussels time and the demo users on Sydney time, so printed session times depend on who reads them.
  • L3Give the demo learner an employee record?demo.learner@example.com has none, so nothing assigned reaches it. Walks use the HR lead instead.
  • L2Fill in Work locations if sites should differ from departmentsThe "site" breakdown uses the Work location, or the department when that is empty. It is empty for everyone today.
  • L2Should the pack notes name Rize?The notes say "Confirm before switching on".
  • L4Hide toolbox talks on people's training page?They show as "taught in a room".
  • L0Should plain employees land in the back office?They use their own training page instead, which now has everything. Changing it is a bigger change to where people land.
  • L7Translate the older employee page words"Your training", the late warning and "Lesson 1 of 3" are still English for Vietnamese readers.
  • L6"Allowance used" looks tiny on payobookThe allowance is counted for every person in the company. Say if it should count only people who claim.

C · Not yet proven live

  • A real phonePhone width was checked in a narrow frame, and the dropped-signal replay was simulated. Nobody has used a real phone, or scanned the QR code with one.
  • "Start the session" and a live QR scanThe demo session is a week away and was left alone, so it does not show as under way. The scan logic was checked on the server.
  • PayAI drafting with a real AI callChecked only against a stand-in. The other three databases correctly say PayAI is not set up.
  • Emails actually reaching peopleMail text, preview and "Send me a test" were checked. No real reminder was sent from the demo.
  • Each person's own browserLive checks used the admin screen plus server-side calls as each demo person.
  • Known failing testsTwo old tests fail on the Assigned tab and have since the first phase. They count the copied database's own rows. Several PayAI tests fail on a machine set to Vietnamese; they pass on live data.

D · Housekeeping

  • Demo passwords sit in older pushed documentsThe same demo passwords appear in several older handover files already on the public repository. The demo database is throwaway, so the risk is low. Rotating them is a short job.
  • "(ESS)" in browser tab titlesAbout ten other modules' employee pages still show it. Not part of Learning.
  • Nothing is pushedThe local branch is far ahead of the shared repository, including other people's payroll work. Push only on your instruction.
  • The Help lessonsHelp now describes the 11 tabs. Any lesson on Learning written later needs the same care.
  • Archived tenantabm is archived and is never deployed to.

7 · How it was checked

Every phase followed the same path before it counted as done.

Before release

  • Automated tests per phase. The last full run on a copy of live data was 1,194 tests with every Learning test passing.
  • A walk in a browser on the engineer's own copy, in English and Vietnamese.
  • Backups of all four databases, then the copy first, then the live ones, one at a time.

After release

  • Matching file contents and version numbers on every database.
  • Scheduled jobs counted before and after, and put back where they were.
  • A live walk of every new screen as the admin, plus read-only server checks as the manager, learner and HR lead, rolled back so nothing changed.
What went wrong along the way, and how it was handled: a usage-limit cut and a network drop interrupted builds twice (resumed, nothing lost). The other deployer's restarts twice looked like bugs and were not. Its combined release of L6 carried unfinished work, failed its own tests and rolled back cleanly; the three causes were fixed. A suspected gap in who could see test results was a test-setup problem, and the page was never open to the wrong people.
