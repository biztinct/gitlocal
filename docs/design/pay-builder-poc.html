Before your first day

Your pay, Asha

A full month, as agreed. Tap a line to see how it is worked out.

Three kinds of line

  • You set: an amount for this person. Type over it. Saved on the contract.
  • Worked out: follows the pay scheme's formula. Locked. Open it to read the sum in words with this person's numbers.
  • Each month: arrives with every month's pay data, like tax. Shown with its usual amount; you can try a number, which is never saved.

Things to try

  • Change the big number and watch every line follow.
  • Point at a worked-out line: the lines it uses light up.
  • Open a worked-out line, then click a name inside the sum.
  • Add an allowance, change it, then press Back to usual.
  • Press Work back from take-home.
  • Empty the big number to see the waiting state.
