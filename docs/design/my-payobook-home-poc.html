My Payobook home, three directions

The page an employee sees first. Today it is a stock portal page: mixed tiles, half with pictures and half without, and nothing personal above the fold. Each option below is the whole page at desktop width. Use the phone toggle to see it at phone width, which is where most employees will open it.

Same for all threeEvery tile gets a coloured Lucide icon, tiles are grouped by life area (Pay, Time, Me, Growth, People), and the finance-style tiles that do not belong to an employee (Invoices, Projects, Tasks) are hidden on employee accounts.
Example figuresThe money, dates and counts shown are sample values to judge the layout, not anyone's real pay.
Recommendation

Option 2, the Bento, with the "Needs you" list from Option 1

Employees open this page for three things: their money, their time off, and a document. Option 2 puts all three on screen with real numbers before a single click. That is the lasting impression. Options 1 and 3 are good pages, but their first screen is still a menu.

  • Option 1 if you want the safest, quickest build.
  • Option 3 if the portal is used mostly on phones and rarely for pay.

Option 1 · Glance

A calm welcome strip with three numbers that matter, a short "Needs you" list, then every tile sorted into groups. Closest to the rest of the product today. The hero moment is the three stat cards.

Option 2 · Bento
