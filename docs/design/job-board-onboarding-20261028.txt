Payobook job-board onboarding — 28 October 2026

For the reviewed application/contact route, specific request and employer steps for
each of the 29 cards, use job-board-partner-applications-20261028.txt in this folder.
That companion guide supersedes the generic outreach drafts below where more
specific or corrected evidence is provided. Neither document records an approval.

Owner walkthrough
1. Open Hiring > Job Boards > Connect & set up. Choose India, Vietnam or Indonesia.
2. Open a provider guide. Payobook owns developer registration, hosting, connector development and certification. The customer owns its employer account and subscription.
3. Copy the prepared request. Send it using your account manager or the official provider contact route. Requests are drafts; none have been sent by this release.
4. Record progress and the location of documents. Do not store secrets in notes.
5. When the provider responds, supply the technical specification and sandbox access through a secure channel. Payobook must complete the adapter and acceptance tests before activation.

What is implemented
- 29 destinations, country/search/method filters, per-company setup progress, manual tracked posting and closure.
- Existing LinkedIn and SEEK APIs remain gated by server-owned partner config and tenant authorization.
- Careerjet and Jooble XML feeds: opt-in per company and role; public advert only; expiry; company/website isolation; disable/re-enable rotates the feed URL.
- Google guidance uses existing careers-page markup. Indexing API notifications are not configured.

Feed walkthrough
1. Confirm the provider accepts your feed, markets and application redirect. Do not buy a subscription without reviewing its terms.
2. Set the correct HTTPS domain on the company website. Enable the feed in the guide and save.
3. Under Publish jobs, choose a public open role, then Manage feed distribution. Choose an expiry date and Include in feed.
4. Copy the feed URL from the provider guide and supply it to the agreed provider.
5. Inclusion is not publication. Verify the external listing, its tracked apply link, and source attribution. Record a live posting only after verification.
6. Removing/closing/expiring a role removes it from the feed; the provider must refresh before its listing disappears.

Partner access still required
There are no approved shared job-board apps configured on the server as inspected for this release. Closed provider contracts are not replaced with guessed endpoints. New partner-only destinations offer manual tracking until an approved API adapter is delivered.
All accounts, contracts, production activation and commercial charges remain subject to owner/provider action.
