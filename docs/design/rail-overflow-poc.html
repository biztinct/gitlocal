The secondary menu, with no scrollbar

Why the People menu scrolls and clips on small screens, and the fix: fewer items, a slightly wider strip, and a fold that keeps every item one click away.

What goes wrong today

  • The strip is 72 px wide and never scrolls the page; when its items are taller than the window, it scrolls inside itself.
  • On a Mac with a mouse plugged in, and on every Windows PC, that inner scrollbar is a real bar 16 px wide. It takes that width from the strip, so the 60 px buttons and 64 px labels get clipped on both sides. That is the cut‑off text in the screenshot. On a trackpad‑only Mac the bar floats and nothing clips, which is why it looks fine on some machines.
  • People has 13 items and Payroll 14; the standard says ten by design, twelve at most. Two of People’s items (Workforce and Insights) are both reports. Payroll carries Reports, Budget and Insights, all reports.
  • Three rows sit above the strip (top bar, area bar, “People › Employees”): 141 px before the first item. On a 768 px‑tall Windows laptop only about 510 px is left: room for nine short items.

The fix, in three layers

1 · Fewer, shorter itemsMerge every report item into Insights (tabs inside). Give the admin items a short word, Access, pinned at the bottom under a hairline. Move Contracts up beside Employees. People 13 → 11 + 1 pinned, Payroll 14 → 11 + 1, Hiring 10 → 8 + 2. 2 · A fold instead of a scrollbarWhen the items do not fit, the ones at the bottom collapse into a More button that opens a small list with full names. The selected item is never hidden. Pinned items fold first. No scrollbar can ever appear, so nothing can ever clip, on any screen, in any area, whatever modules a company has. 3 · A little more width72 → 80 px. Today the longest words (WORKFORCE, CONTRACTS, DELEGATION) measure 61–62 px in a 64 px box. At 80 px the label gets a 72 px box, so every English and Vietnamese word fits with room to spare, and the letters can go from 9 px to 10 px for readability. The page loses 8 px of width, about half a percent. Optional · One row less on top“People › Employees” repeats what the strip and the dark bar already say. Folding it into the dark bar frees 45 px on every screen: one more item visible. Tick the box above to see it.
