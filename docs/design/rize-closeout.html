Connecting the real Zoho later

The door is built and tested with sample data. When you have Zoho admin access, point its webhook here. The key lives on the connector called "Zoho People — inbound" in Integrations.

POST https://payobook.com/api/zoho/webhook

                 "Dateofjoining": "YYYY-MM-DD", "Employeestatus": "Active" } ] }

Arrivals land in the company set on that connector (currently Payobook Vietnam JSC — an editable field). The Arrival rules table decides what each status word does: open a joining checklist, open an exit, update the record, or park it for review. Sending Payobook data back to Zoho was deliberately left out, as agreed.

Two money rules to remember

Awards reach a payslip only when a person presses Queue on the Awards panel, after a preview. The pay run must still be at the start of its approval chain, and its pay scheme needs an input component coded INCENTV — otherwise the screen refuses and says why. Awards mark themselves Paid when the run's final approval lands.

A final settlement cannot be closed while equipment is out, a department clearance is pending, or a blocking exit task is open. The refusal names every blocker.
