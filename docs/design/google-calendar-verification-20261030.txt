Requested scopes for new authorizations:
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.owned
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.freebusy

Implementation: pb_hiring extends google.service._get_authorize_uri. Standard
Odoo Calendar's full-calendar scope is replaced at that registry hook; standard
addons are preserved. Read-only requests remain read-only. Other Google services
and extra explicitly requested scopes are preserved. Existing tokens are neither
revoked nor changed: previously granted broad permissions are not automatically
reduced. A clean grant may require the user to remove the existing Payobook grant
in their Google account and reconnect. Do this for a controlled test account,
not for all production users automatically.

Connection management (2026-10-30 follow-up): Calendar > Connect checks the
saved Google connection when Connect / reconnect or Check connection is pressed.
A valid connection shows the primary calendar title returned by Google and does
not open OAuth. This title is not asserted to be a verified account email.
Expired access tokens refresh silently; revoked access offers a new consent
flow. Temporary Google/network failures keep the saved connection and report
that it could not be checked. Remove connection explicitly forgets only the
current user's saved credentials in Payobook and stops sync; it does not revoke
the project-wide grant, remove another user's connection, or delete meetings.
Normal sync is unchanged. Only an explicit new/repair connection asks Google
for account selection and consent, with the same two narrow Calendar scopes.

For a demo with an already connected test account: choose Remove connection in
Payobook and confirm before recording. Start recording, click Connect /
reconnect, then Continue to Google. Do not reset production users' connections
to prepare the demo. Removing the local connection does not itself remove old
broader grants on Google's side; the controlled pilot grants were separately
revoked by the owner. Use the same controlled test account for the recording.

Availability identity correction (2026-10-30): Google Hiring connection checks
and panel availability query the connected organiser's primary calendar, not
the organiser's Payobook login/work email. If the organiser is also selected
on the panel, that employee is recognised by user_id and is not queried again
using their work email. Other panellists still require calendar addresses and
availability sharing; missing/inaccessible calendars must never count as free.
Microsoft identifiers are unchanged. No additional OAuth scope is required.

Availability demo route: Calendar > Find a time > Choose a candidate opens
Hiring's applicant board. Open a controlled DEMO candidate, then Find panel
availability in the candidate profile. Choose Google Calendar and a panel whose
calendars the connected account can read, choose dates, duration and timezone,
then Find shared availability. This lists slots and prepares a private booking
link; it does not send an invitation. Do not share that link in a demonstration.
Hiring connections must first pass its real Google check and be enabled for
the pilot company. Each organiser still needs their own Google consent.

The connector reads/inserts/patches/deletes primary-calendar events, watches
primary event changes, and queries participant free/busy availability. It does
not require calendar ACL management or deletion of whole calendars.

Justification (under the Google form's 1000-character limit):
Payobook provides meeting and interview scheduling with optional Google Calendar synchronisation. After a user connects their account, calendar.events.owned lets Payobook read, create, update and delete events on that user's primary calendar, keeping meeting times, attendees and cancellations synchronised. Read-only access cannot save scheduling changes, and app-created-calendar access cannot synchronise the user's existing primary calendar. calendar.events.freebusy lets the scheduling feature check available times for participants whose availability the connected user is permitted to view, without retrieving their event details. Availability alone cannot support event synchronisation. Payobook does not need permission to change calendar sharing or delete entire calendars.
