What the search changes—and what it protects

The search varies operator hiring, specialists, team leads, overtime and assumed productivity on a bounded grid. It keeps your demand, stress, start month, office hiring, salary increase, shifts, absence, contributions and deductions. Existing 240 jobs are protected; planned additions may change. Each lane shows its least-cost feasible candidate. If none meets every selected goal, the result is labeled and ranked by fewest unmet goals, normalized shortfall, then cost. Suggestions are model calculations, not AI forecasts. The hiring lane holds current productivity; the development lane holds operator hiring. Lanes may reach the same solution.
