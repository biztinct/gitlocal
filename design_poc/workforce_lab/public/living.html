How these suggestions work

This demo checks 2,496 combinations of hiring, support roles, overtime and assumed productivity, with hires starting in February. Each lane selects its lowest-cost feasible candidate. An infeasible cost target shows its lowest-cost candidate; other infeasible targets show highest coverage. This is a bounded model search, not AI or a guarantee of the best possible plan. The search uses the original demo assumptions, including an unstressed demand forecast; previewing a path replaces your current assumptions.
